A mantra (thần chú) is a recitation text with a name and a full text body.
It may optionally be linked to one [bodhisattva](/glossary/bodhisattva.md)
via `bodhisattva_id`, and may optionally have a [sect](/glossary/sect.md)
and a recitation audio URL.

Used in [Mantra List](/features/mantra-list.md) and
[Mantra Detail](/features/mantra-detail.md); referenced from
[Bodhisattva Detail](/features/bodhisattva-detail.md).
