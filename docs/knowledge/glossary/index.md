# Glossary

* [Sutra](sutra.md) - A Buddhist scripture (kinh điển) with full text, optional sect, and optional chanting audio.
* [Mantra](mantra.md) - A recitation text (thần chú) optionally linked to a bodhisattva and a sect.
* [Bodhisattva](bodhisattva.md) - A Buddha or Bodhisattva profile (Chư Phật & Bồ Tát) with a description, image, and related mantras.
* [Sect](sect.md) - A Buddhist tradition (tông phái) a sutra, mantra, or bodhisattva may belong to.
* [Delta Sync](delta-sync.md) - The offline-first sync strategy shared by sutra, mantra, and bodhisattva lists.
