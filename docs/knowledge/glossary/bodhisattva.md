A bodhisattva entry (Chư Phật & Bồ Tát) represents a Buddha or Bodhisattva
in the content library: a name, a description, an optional image URL, an
optional [sect](/glossary/sect.md), and zero or more related
[mantras](/glossary/mantra.md).

A bodhisattva with no sect assigned is one venerated across multiple
sects; it only appears under "All", not under a specific sect filter.

Used in [Bodhisattva List](/features/bodhisattva-list.md) and
[Bodhisattva Detail](/features/bodhisattva-detail.md).
